资格与路径Eligibility & pathways

我应该先了解哪一类美国移民？Which U.S. pathway should I explore first?＋

先从个人成就、专业经历、赴美计划、雇主安排与投资意愿梳理。不同类别的证据逻辑不同，不能仅凭职位、学历或预算决定方向。Start with achievements, experience, U.S. plans, employer arrangements and investment considerations. A title, degree or budget alone cannot select a pathway.

EB-1A 和 NIW 有什么不同？How do EB-1A and NIW differ?＋

EB-1A 侧重杰出能力与持续认可；NIW 须先满足 EB-2 基础资格，再论证事业与国家利益豁免。两者可由符合条件的申请人自行申请，但判断框架并不相同。EB-1A focuses on extraordinary ability and sustained recognition. NIW requires underlying EB-2 eligibility and a case for the national-interest waiver. Eligible applicants may self-petition, but the frameworks differ.

没有美国雇主，是否还有职业移民方向？Can a route be considered without a U.S. employer?＋

符合条件的 EB-1A 可自行申请，NIW 可请求豁免工作邀请和劳工认证要求。无需雇主不代表无需资格、证据或后续程序。Eligible EB-1A applicants may self-petition, and NIW can waive job-offer and labor-certification requirements. This does not waive eligibility, evidence or later procedures.

时间与费用Timing & costs

I-140 获批是否就等于拿到绿卡？Does I-140 approval mean I have a green card?＋

不是。I-140 是相关职业移民请愿的一部分；还需核对签证名额及后续移民签证或适用身份调整程序。No. I-140 approval is one part of the process; visa availability and subsequent immigrant-visa or applicable adjustment procedures must also be addressed.

为什么不能直接承诺办理周期？Why is there no fixed completion promise?＋

资格请愿、排期与后续程序是不同环节；补件、个案情况及主管部门安排也会影响进度。规划应采用可更新的节点，而不是固定获批日期。Petitions, visa queues and later procedures are separate, and evidence requests and agency operations may affect timing. Plan around updateable milestones, not a fixed approval date.

继续了解 / LEARN MORE →
加急处理能跳过排期吗？Does premium processing bypass the visa queue?＋

不能。加急只针对指定申请的审理行动，不保证批准，也不消除签证名额等待。No. Premium processing concerns adjudicative action on specified filings; it does not guarantee approval or remove visa-number waits.

继续了解 / LEARN MORE →
EB-5 的投资额是否就是全部费用？Is the EB-5 investment the entire cost?＋

不是。投资本金之外，还需分别了解政府费用、专业服务费、项目相关费用及其他支出，并独立评估投资风险。No. Review government, professional, project-related and other costs separately from capital, together with investment risk.

家庭与生活Family & life

配偶和孩子是否可以一起规划？Can my spouse and children be included?＋

符合条件的配偶与子女可能作为衍生申请人，但关系、婚姻状态、年龄及具体程序需要逐一核对。Qualifying spouses and children may be derivative applicants, but relationships, marital status, age and procedures must be checked individually.

孩子接近二十一岁需要关注什么？What if my child is approaching 21?＋

应提前评估 CSPA 适用、年龄计算、签证可用性和后续行动期限；不要假定最初递交日即可永久锁龄。Review CSPA eligibility, age calculation, visa availability and action deadlines early. Do not assume the initial filing date permanently freezes age.

移民后税务、教育和安居也能一起讨论吗？Should tax, schooling and relocation be considered?＋

这些事项可以纳入规划清单，但各自适用不同规则。具体税务判断、入学条件及交易安排，应分别核对并取得合适的专业意见。Include them in the planning checklist, while verifying their separate rules and obtaining appropriate tax, education or transaction advice.

新加坡 EP 是否可以自动转 PR？Does a Singapore EP automatically become PR?＋

不会。EP 是工作准证，PR 由 ICA 按申请类别与个人情况另行评估，持有 EP 或达到某一收入不保证 PR 获批。No. EP is a work pass, while ICA separately assesses PR. Holding an EP or reaching a particular income does not guarantee PR.

第一次咨询需要把所有材料都交齐吗？Do I need every document for a first consultation?＋

一般可先沟通背景、家庭情况、目标与时间预期，再确定所需材料及服务范围。请先确认收件方式，再提供敏感文件。Begin with your background, family, goals and timing, then clarify documents and scope. Confirm the delivery method before providing sensitive records.
