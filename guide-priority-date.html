排期、审批与加急处理有什么区别？Priority Dates, Processing and Premium Service

TIMING & PLANNING · 2026-09-22

处理时间不是全过程时间Processing time is not the entire journey

USCIS 的处理时间反映特定申请的审理情况，并非最终取得绿卡的承诺日期。补件、案件特点与主管部门工作安排都可能影响实际进度。USCIS processing times describe a particular filing, not a promised green-card date. Evidence requests, case characteristics and agency operations may affect progress.

排期反映签证名额可用性Priority dates and visa availability

受年度名额限制的类别还需关注 Visa Bulletin。应核对类别、适用国别、优先日期及当期图表规则；境内申请还需查看 USCIS 当月允许采用的图表。不要只根据国籍或单项批复判断可否继续。Numerically limited categories require a review of the Visa Bulletin: category, chargeability, priority date and applicable chart. Adjustment applicants also need the USCIS chart selection for that month. Nationality or a single approval alone is not enough.

加急不会消除排期Premium processing does not remove a queue

加急处理适用于指定申请，要求在规定期限内采取审理行动；行动可能是批准、拒绝、补件或拟拒通知。它不保证批准，也不直接缩短签证名额等待。Premium processing applies to specified filings and requires adjudicative action within the applicable period. That action can include approval, denial or an evidence request; it neither guarantees approval nor removes visa-number waits.
