从申请到绿卡：先分清关键环节From Petition to Permanent Residence

PROCESS BASICS · 2026-09-22

先判断类别与证据Eligibility and evidence

首先讨论个人背景与拟申请类别的匹配度，并确定哪些事实已有证明、哪些内容需要补充。项目名称、宣传口号或单一学历都不能替代证据审查。Start by assessing the fit between the individual and the category, distinguishing documented facts from gaps. A label, slogan or degree does not replace evidence review.

资格请愿与后续程序Petition and subsequent procedures

不同路径可能先涉及劳工认证或移民请愿。相关请愿获批之后，还需结合签证名额可用性及个人情况，判断通过领事程序还是符合条件的境内身份调整继续推进。A route may involve labor certification or an immigrant petition first. After petition approval, visa availability and individual circumstances determine the appropriate consular or eligible adjustment-of-status procedure.

签证、在美身份与绿卡Visa, status and green card

签证通常用于赴美口岸申请入境，不保证获准入境；在美获准停留期限和条件需结合 I-94 及具体身份规则查看。绿卡是永久居民身份的证明，三者不能混用。A visa generally permits travel to seek admission, not guaranteed entry. Authorized stay and conditions depend on I-94 and status rules. A green card evidences permanent residence; these concepts are different.
