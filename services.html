JP GOLDEN PEARL · OUR APPROACH

服务流程Our Approach

从第一次交流到明确后续安排，让每一步都有清楚的议题、资料与目标。具体服务内容与交付范围以双方确认的约定为准。From the first conversation to the next agreed steps, keep the questions, documents and objectives clear. Specific services and deliverables are subject to the agreed scope.

01

了解背景与目标Understand your starting point

沟通个人经历、职业方向、家庭成员、目的地偏好与时间预期。首次交流以必要的信息为主，不必一次准备所有证明文件。Discuss your background, career, family, destination preferences and timing. An initial conversation can begin with essential information.

02

梳理方向与关键问题Compare pathways and key questions

根据实际情况，整理需要了解的申请类别、资格条件、材料线索与不确定因素。区分已知事实与仍需核实的内容。Identify relevant categories, requirements, evidence and uncertainties, separating known facts from matters requiring verification.

03

明确准备与服务范围Define preparation and scope

讨论资料准备顺序、沟通节点、工作分工及费用构成。需要个案法律或税务意见的事项，单独列出并确认处理方式。Discuss document priorities, milestones, responsibilities and cost categories. Identify matters requiring individual legal or tax advice.

04

沟通进展与后续安排Review developments and next steps

当个人情况、政策信息或时间计划发生变化时，重新梳理相关问题；并将家属、搬迁与生活准备纳入后续讨论。Revisit questions when personal circumstances, policy information or timing change, including family, relocation and practical preparation.

有用的首次咨询，不是立即给出一个结果，而是共同确定下一步需要查清什么、准备什么。A useful first consultation identifies what needs to be clarified and prepared next, rather than promising an immediate outcome.
