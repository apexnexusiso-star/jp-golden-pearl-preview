美国移民概览U.S. Immigration Pathways

美国职业移民有不同的资格框架。选择方向时，应先梳理个人成就、专业背景、赴美发展计划、雇主安排与投资意愿，再讨论适用类别。U.S. employment-based immigration includes different eligibility frameworks. Begin with achievements, professional background, U.S. plans, employer arrangements and investment considerations.

先分清三件事Three separate questions

  • 资格是否匹配：您的事实与证据，是否支持所考虑的类别。Eligibility: whether the facts and evidence support the category being considered.

  • 申请如何推进：资格请愿、名额可用性及后续手续属于不同环节。Process: the petition, visa availability and subsequent procedures are separate stages.

  • 家庭如何安排：配偶、子女年龄、工作与出入境计划需要一并考虑。Family: consider spouses, children’s ages, work and travel plans alongside the principal case.

类别名称不能替代个案判断；I-140 获批也不等于已经取得永久居民身份。A category name does not replace an individual assessment. An approved I-140 is not itself permanent resident status.
