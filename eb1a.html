EB-1A 杰出人才Extraordinary Ability

类别定位What the category addresses

EB-1A 属于美国职业移民第一优先类别中的杰出人才方向，涉及科学、艺术、教育、商业或体育等领域。评估重点包括持续的国家级或国际认可，以及能够支持该领域杰出能力的实际证据。EB-1A addresses extraordinary ability in fields such as science, arts, education, business and athletics. Assessment concerns sustained national or international recognition and supporting evidence.

咨询前可以梳理什么Useful preparation

可先整理个人履历、代表性成果、获得认可的记录、作品或研究、业务贡献及其证明来源。不同职业的证据形态可能不同，材料应真实、可核验，并与申请人的具体贡献相对应。Prepare a career history, representative achievements, recognition, work or research, business contributions and their supporting sources. Evidence should be authentic, verifiable and tied to the individual’s contribution.

需要避免的误解Common misunderstandings

EB-1A 不是仅针对学术人员，也不能仅凭某个头衔、奖项或材料数量判断结果。符合条件者可以自行申请，但仍须证明符合类别要求，并说明赴美继续在相关领域工作的计划。EB-1A is not limited to academics. A title, award or quantity of documents cannot determine the result. Eligible individuals may self-petition but must satisfy the category and plan to continue work in the relevant field.

初次咨询建议准备个人履历、家庭基本情况和希望实现的目标。涉及具体资格判断时，需要对完整事实与证明材料作进一步审查。For an initial consultation, prepare your résumé, basic family circumstances and goals. Individual eligibility requires further review of complete facts and evidence.
