公司简介About JP Golden Pearl

立足新加坡，连接全球人生规划A Singapore perspective. A global outlook.

JP Golden Pearl Consulting Pte. Ltd.（金珠移民咨询）是一家立足新加坡、聚焦美国与新加坡移民及身份规划咨询的公司。我们面向希望拓展国际发展空间、规划家庭未来与了解海外生活选择的客户，围绕个人背景、专业经历、家庭结构与长期目标，提供有条理的咨询沟通。JP Golden Pearl Consulting Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based consultancy focused on U.S. immigration and Singapore residency planning. We help clients explore international opportunities in the context of their professional background, family circumstances and long-term goals.

我们的工作从理解客户开始：希望在哪里发展事业，如何安排家人的生活，何时启动准备，以及哪些问题需要优先解决。通过移民信息梳理、路径比较、准备事项沟通与后续安排讨论，将复杂的信息转化为更清晰的决策依据。We begin by understanding where clients want to build their careers, how they envision family life, when they wish to prepare and which questions matter most. We structure information, compare potential routes and discuss preparation and next steps.

以事实为基础，以长期目标为方向Clarity, context and long-term thinking

移民规划既关乎申请，也关乎申请之后的生活。金珠重视每一项信息的依据、每一步安排的适配性，以及咨询过程中的清晰沟通。我们不把某一个项目视为所有人的答案，而是帮助客户识别不同选择的前提、准备成本与关键不确定因素。Immigration planning extends beyond an application. We value evidence, suitability and clear communication, helping clients understand the prerequisites, preparation costs and uncertainties of different options rather than treating one pathway as a universal answer.

  • 个人化沟通：围绕真实经历和家庭需求展开，而非仅以职称、学历或预算下结论。Individual context: consider actual experience and family needs, beyond a job title, degree or budget.

  • 清晰的准备思路：把可核验材料、时间节点和需要确认的问题分开梳理。Structured preparation: distinguish verifiable evidence, milestones and outstanding questions.

  • 明确的服务边界：根据实际需求确认工作内容；涉及个案法律、税务或投资判断时，另行寻求相应专业意见。Clear scope: agree the work required and obtain appropriately qualified legal, tax or investment advice when needed.

专注的业务方向Our focus

美国业务内容涵盖 EB-1A、EB-2 NIW、EB-5 及雇主担保等类别的信息咨询与规划沟通；新加坡业务内容围绕工作准证、永久居民与家庭长期安排展开。具体是否适用，应结合个人情况、现行政策与进一步的专业评估确认。Our U.S. consultation topics include EB-1A, EB-2 NIW, EB-5 and employer-sponsored routes. Singapore topics include employment passes, permanent residence and long-term family arrangements. Suitability depends on individual circumstances, current rules and further professional assessment.
