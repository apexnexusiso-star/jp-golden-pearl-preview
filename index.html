QUESTIONS THAT MATTER | 您关心的问题

EB-1A 和 NIW 有什么不同？How do EB-1A and NIW differ?＋

EB-1A 侧重杰出能力与持续认可；NIW 须先满足 EB-2 基础资格，再论证事业与国家利益豁免。两者可由符合条件的申请人自行申请，但判断框架并不相同。EB-1A focuses on extraordinary ability and sustained recognition. NIW requires underlying EB-2 eligibility and a case for the national-interest waiver. Eligible applicants may self-petition, but the frameworks differ.

为什么不能直接承诺办理周期？Why is there no fixed completion promise?＋

资格请愿、排期与后续程序是不同环节；补件、个案情况及主管部门安排也会影响进度。规划应采用可更新的节点，而不是固定获批日期。Petitions, visa queues and later procedures are separate, and evidence requests and agency operations may affect timing. Plan around updateable milestones, not a fixed approval date.

孩子接近二十一岁需要关注什么？What if my child is approaching 21?＋

应提前评估 CSPA 适用、年龄计算、签证可用性和后续行动期限；不要假定最初递交日即可永久锁龄。Review CSPA eligibility, age calculation, visa availability and action deadlines early. Do not assume the initial filing date permanently freezes age.
