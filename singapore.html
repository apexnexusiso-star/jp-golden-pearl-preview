新加坡身份规划Singapore Residency Planning

在新加坡工作、经营事业与取得长期居留身份，属于相互关联但不同的规划议题。了解主管部门、申请类别及实际生活目标，是建立合理预期的第一步。Working in Singapore, developing a business and obtaining long-term residence are related but distinct questions. Begin with the responsible authority, category and practical goals.

Employment Pass：工作准证Employment and professional development

EP 由新加坡人力部 MOM 管理，面向符合条件的外国专业人士、经理等。准证资格、薪资与评估要求以 MOM 当前规定为准；工作准证与永久居民身份并不等同。The Employment Pass is administered by MOM for eligible foreign professionals, managers and related roles. Current salary and assessment requirements must be checked with MOM; an employment pass is not permanent residence.

Permanent Residence：永久居民A separate assessment by ICA

EP 持有人属于可申请 PR 的群体之一，但不存在自动转换。ICA 会综合考虑家庭联系、经济贡献、资历、年龄、家庭构成和居住时间等因素，评估与新加坡的联系及长期扎根意愿。EP holders are among the groups eligible to apply for PR, but there is no automatic conversion. ICA considers factors including family ties, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile and length of residence.

家庭与长期安排Planning beyond an application

家庭规划应同时讨论配偶与子女安排、教育及生活需求，以及适用的再入境许可和男性家庭成员国民服役要求。商业投资者如希望了解 GIP，应另行核对 EDB 官方条件，而不将其与一般工作准证混为一谈。Discuss spouses, children, education and practical needs, together with applicable re-entry permit and National Service obligations. Business investors exploring GIP should separately review EDB’s criteria rather than equating it with a standard employment-pass route.
