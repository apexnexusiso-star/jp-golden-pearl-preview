EB-5 投资移民Immigrant Investor Programme

投资金额只是起点The threshold is only a starting point

适用现行门槛的新申请，标准最低投资额为 105 万美元；符合条件的目标就业区或区域中心基础设施项目可适用 80 万美元门槛。两者均须核对项目适用条件；投资额不包含全部服务及申请费用。For new filings subject to current thresholds, the standard minimum is US$1,050,000; qualifying targeted-employment-area or regional-center infrastructure investments may use US$800,000. Applicable conditions must be checked, and the investment is not the total cost.

资金与就业要求Capital and job creation

申请还涉及合格企业、资金的合法来源及流转记录，以及至少十个合格全职岗位等要求。评估时应将资金证明、就业依据与项目文件结合起来，而不是只看项目宣传中的预测。The case also involves a qualifying enterprise, lawful source and path of funds, and at least ten qualifying full-time jobs. Review the supporting records and project documents rather than relying solely on promotional forecasts.

投资风险与身份程序分开评估Separate investment risk from immigration procedure

EB-5 资本需要承担投资风险，不能承诺返本或移民获批。完成相应程序后通常先取得两年有条件永久居民身份，再通过 I-829 申请解除条件，仍需证明满足相关要求。EB-5 capital must be at risk; repayment and immigration approval cannot be guaranteed. The process generally leads first to two-year conditional permanent residence, followed by an I-829 petition to remove conditions.

初次咨询建议准备个人履历、家庭基本情况和希望实现的目标。涉及具体资格判断时，需要对完整事实与证明材料作进一步审查。For an initial consultation, prepare your résumé, basic family circumstances and goals. Individual eligibility requires further review of complete facts and evidence.
