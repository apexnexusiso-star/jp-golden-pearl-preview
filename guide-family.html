配偶与孩子，如何一起纳入规划？Planning for Spouses and Children

FAMILY & FUTURE · 2026-09-22

每位家庭成员都需要核对条件Review each family member

部分职业移民允许符合条件的配偶及未婚、未满二十一岁子女作为衍生申请人随同或随后团聚。主申请人的资格成立，不自动证明所有家庭成员均符合条件。Certain employment-based routes allow qualifying spouses and unmarried children under 21 to accompany or follow the principal applicant. The principal’s eligibility does not automatically establish every family member’s eligibility.

子女年龄要提前评估Review children’s ages early

孩子接近年龄界限时，应单独评估 CSPA。适用的年龄计算、签证可用性和后续行动要求可能影响结果；递交时未满二十一岁并非永久锁龄。2025 年相关规则已有调整，需结合申请日期与具体程序复核。For children nearing the limit, assess CSPA separately. Age calculations, visa availability and required subsequent actions can affect eligibility. Filing before 21 does not permanently freeze age. Relevant policy changed in 2025, so the filing date and process matter.

把生活安排与申请节点衔接Connect application milestones with daily life

可先建立家庭清单：成员出生日期与婚姻状态、现有签证或身份、申请记录、教育计划及预期搬迁时间。涉及年龄保护、监护或出入境安排时，取得针对个案的专业判断。Prepare a family timeline covering birth dates, marital status, current visas or status, application history, education and relocation goals. Obtain individual professional advice on age protection, custody or travel issues.
