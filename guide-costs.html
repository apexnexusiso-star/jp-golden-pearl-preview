费用与材料：首次咨询前准备什么？Costs and Documents: Prepare for a Useful Conversation

COSTS & PREPARATION · 2026-09-22

按费用性质拆分预算Separate the components of a budget

预算通常需要区分政府申请费、专业服务费、翻译与公证等文件费用，以及可能涉及的体检、旅行和搬迁支出。投资路径还需单独列示投资本金及相关成本，不能把最低投资额当作总费用。Separate government filing fees, professional fees, translation and document costs, and possible medical, travel and relocation expenses. For investor routes, show investment capital and associated costs separately rather than treating a minimum investment as the total budget.

先准备信息摘要，再补齐证明Start with a summary, then assemble evidence

首次交流可准备履历、学历与职业经历摘要、家庭成员与年龄、曾有的申请记录、目的地及时间目标。原始证明可按后续需要整理，避免在尚未明确要求前重复翻译或制作材料。Begin with a résumé, education and work summary, family members and ages, prior applications, destination and timing goals. Assemble underlying records according to the subsequent requirements, avoiding unnecessary early translation or document work.

确认报价包含什么Clarify what a quotation includes

确认服务范围、交付内容、付款节点、第三方费用及可能产生的追加工作。涉及投资、合同或税务安排时，分别取得相应意见，并保留书面约定与费用记录。Clarify the scope, deliverables, payment milestones, third-party charges and possible additional work. Obtain appropriate advice on investments, contracts or taxes and retain written agreements and fee records.
